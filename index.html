TechTronix Innovations Power BI

Sales & Inventory Optimization Analysis

The client had extensive data but no clear path from it to decisions. I built a Power BI analysis layer that streamlined inventory for profitability, surfaced customer-feedback signals for strategic improvement, and identified untapped market-expansion opportunities.

Inventory strategy Profitability Market expansion
  • Industrial is the most profitable customer sector, ahead of Consumer Electronics and Automotive.
  • Asia-Pacific leads all regions in profitability, followed closely by Europe and North America.
  • Products 1100, 1469, and 1315 carry the highest production cost — the clearest candidates for a cost-reduction review.
  • Robotics revenue concentrates in Europe and Asia-Pacific, while Sensors perform strongest in North America.
  • $252M total revenue against $53M total profit and 501K units sold across the full dataset.

Tools: Microsoft Power BI Desktop.

Data: A year of Techtronix Innovations transaction data — sales, customer interactions, product specifics, and financial metrics — compiled into a single analysis-ready dataset.

Process: Built a four-page report (Sales Analysis, Customer Insights, Inventory Management & Profitability, Expansion) with cross-filtering slicers for sector, country, product category, and region, plus a decomposition-tree visual for drilling from total revenue down to individual regions.

Delivered: Strategic recommendations to prioritize Asia-Pacific growth, strengthen the Industrial sector, review negative-margin products, and implement demand forecasting to close the gap between production and sales.

FlexTrade Excel

UX Analysis for Conversion Recovery

Strong downloads and active users weren't converting. I diagnosed the funnel in Excel — isolating elevated bounce rate, cart abandonment, and below-target order value — and delivered checkout and personalization recommendations to close the gap.

Funnel diagnostics Checkout UX Personalization
  • Cart-addition bugs and checkout friction were the two most-cited pain points across all four years of customer feedback.
  • Bounce rate consistently peaked on weekends, pointing to a traffic-quality issue rather than a site-wide problem.
  • Average session duration held steady at ~15 minutes even as conversion rate fluctuated year to year — an engagement-to-conversion gap, not an engagement problem.
  • Recommended checkout streamlining and personalization directly targeted the two lowest-scoring stages of the funnel.

Tools: Microsoft Excel — pivot tables, slicers, and conditional formatting.

Data: FlexTrade shopping-app user-interaction and feedback data, fiscal years 2020–2023.

Business problem: Strong downloads and active users weren’t converting — high bounce rate, cart abandonment, and a lower-than-target average order value, against heavy competition from other shopping apps.

Process: Built a year-filterable interactive dashboard (KPI cards, bounce/conversion trend, session-duration analysis, weekday bounce-rate breakdown, cart-addition trend, and a ranked customer-feedback chart), then delivered a phased action plan — bug-fix sprint, search overhaul, checkout redesign, user segmentation, content audit, and continuous A/B testing.

DigitalPath Innovations Tableau

A/B Testing for UX Optimization

Ran a structured A/B test across two webpage variants, comparing engagement, conversion-funnel efficiency, content interaction, and referral-source impact in Tableau to identify the higher-performing variant and guide the next round of UX changes.

Experiment design Funnel efficiency Referral impact
  • Version A drove a dramatically higher conversion rate (70.5% vs 38.2%) despite a similar checkout-analysis score — the funnels diverge sharply after checkout.
  • Version A users spent nearly 50% more time on site (5.0 vs 3.3 minutes) and viewed twice as many pages (10 vs 5).
  • Version B had a marginally higher cart-addition rate (72.6% vs 71.5%), but that didn't translate into more conversions.
  • Search-engine traffic favored Version B (186 vs 151 users), while direct and social traffic favored Version A.

Tools: Tableau.

Data: A two-week randomized A/B test capturing user interactions, behaviors, and responses to two versions (A and B) of DigitalPath Innovations’ webpage.

Process: Built a single consolidated dashboard covering KPIs, checkout analysis, conversion rate, feedback analysis, cart addition, content interaction by traffic channel, and time-on-site — comparing both versions side by side throughout.

Delivered: Six strategic recommendations, including a root-cause analysis of Version B’s lower engagement, mapping Version A’s higher-converting funnel to replicate its drivers, and a full audit of the cart-to-checkout drop-off.

NYC Traffic Safety Analysis

Cleaned and structured three years of NYC collision records in Excel, then used pivot tables and conditional formatting to surface high-risk locations and peak-hour patterns — tracing the leading contributors back to human factors like distracted driving over environmental ones.

Data cleaning Pivot tables Risk pattern analysis
  • A small number of locations account for a disproportionate share of collisions — pointing to targeted intervention over city-wide policy.
  • Collision frequency peaks during congested traffic hours, tying accident risk directly to volume, not just road design.
  • Distracted driving and failure to yield outranked environmental factors as leading contributors — a behavioral, not infrastructural, root cause.
  • Delivered as an interactive pivot-table dashboard so non-analysts can filter by year, location, or severity without touching raw data.
